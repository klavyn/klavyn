Chorded typing · no special hardware

klavyn

Press a word's letters at once — get the whole word.

A QWERTY chorded-typing trainer. Hold or roll the letters in any order; klavyn recognizes the chord and types the word. The CharaChorder idea, in software, on the keyboard you already have.

what a chord looks like

k l o → look

Three keys, pressed together or rolled fast — one word out. No seed letters, no separate theory to learn: just the word's own letters.

⇄

Hold or roll

Mash the letters at once, or roll through them one at a time within the roll-gap window. Long words work even on keyboards with a low simultaneous-key ceiling.

文

Every language

Dictionaries ship for many languages, with a community-extensible abbreviation tier layered on top of the per-language defaults.

◈

Local & private

Everything runs on your machine. No network calls, no disk writes of what you type. It's open source — read SECURITY.md and verify.
